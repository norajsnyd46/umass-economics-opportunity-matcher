How recommendations are matched
Scoring model: Each answer adds weighted points to five pathways: internship, research, campus organization, volunteering/advocacy, and advising. The highest-scoring pathway is recommended, with feasibility adjustments for time, experience, paid-work needs, and timing. The tool does not determine actual eligibility or live openings.

Important distinctions: EURA is for Economics majors and requires a faculty mentor; PAES is a Resource Economics student organization open to people interested in applied economics; the Experimental Economics Lab recruits participants for paid experiments, which is different from a research assistant job. Students should confirm current openings, schedules, and requirements on each official page.
